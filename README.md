# Sales Performance & Customer Segmentation Dashboard | Microsoft Excel

## 1. Project Overview

An interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard developed to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segmentation, and order trends.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue generation, profit contribution, customer purchasing behavior, shipping preferences, and discount impact to support data-driven business decisions.

## 2. Business Problem

Understanding sales performance requires more than tracking total revenue. Businesses also need to evaluate profitability, identify valuable customers, understand customer segments, and assess how pricing and discount strategies influence purchasing behavior.

This project addresses the following business questions:

- How are sales and profits performing overall?
- Who are the highest-value customers in terms of sales and profit?
- Which months generate the highest order volumes?
- Which customer segments contribute most to sales?
- What shipping methods do customers prefer?
- How do discount levels relate to order quantities?
- Are high sales volumes always associated with higher profitability?

## 3. Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales and profitability.
- Identify high-value customers based on sales and profit contribution.
- Analyze monthly order trends and seasonal patterns.
- Understand customer segment distribution.
- Evaluate shipping method preferences.
- Explore the relationship between discounts and purchasing quantities.
- Provide insights that support customer, pricing, and sales strategies.

## 5. Analysis & Key Findings

### Sales & Profitability Analysis
- Analyzed overall sales and profit performance.
- Compared sales volume and net profit across the Top 10 customers to identify high-value clients.

### Monthly Order Trends
- Analyzed order fluctuations throughout the year.
- Identified November and December as peak months in order activity.

### Customer Segmentation
- Analyzed sales distribution across customer segments.
- The Consumer segment represents the largest share, contributing approximately 51%.

### Shipping Preferences
- Standard Class is the most frequently utilized shipping method.

### Discount Impact
- Explored the relationship between discount rates and order quantities among top customers.
- Examined how discounting relates to purchasing behavior.

## 7. Business Recommendations

1. **Customer Strategy:** Develop targeted strategies for high-value customers based on their sales and profit contributions.
2. **Seasonal Planning:** Prepare inventory and sales operations for peak demand periods in November and December.
3. **Segment Strategy:** Prioritize the Consumer segment while exploring growth opportunities in other segments.
4. **Discount Optimization:** Evaluate discount effectiveness by comparing additional sales volume against profit margins.
5. **Shipping Strategy:** Maintain efficient Standard Class operations while monitoring customer needs across other shipping options.
6. **Profitability Management:** Focus on profitable sales rather than revenue growth alone.
